# Security Hall of Fame

We thank the security researchers and community members who help keep
DRS AI safe by responsibly disclosing vulnerabilities.

## Recognition Criteria

Reporters are listed here when:

1. The report describes a real, reproducible vulnerability.
2. The report is delivered through the responsible disclosure process
   defined in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
3. The issue is confirmed and fixed by the maintainers.

Listing is optional and requires the reporter's consent; anonymous
reports are honored on request.
